Fossbook keeps the script and artwork references together in a readable Markdown
file. Start with a normal image, then place its dialogue immediately beneath it.

![A cartoonist assembles colorful blocks into a larger structure.](/fossbook/images/fossbook-comic-authoring.png "size:75% A Fossbook story starts as portable source files.")

> “The story is just Markdown?” \
> “Markdown, artwork, and the details readers need.”

The alternative text describes the scene, the title becomes a caption, and the
blockquote becomes an optional transcript. Select **Display dialogue text** above
to see how readers control that transcript.
